About Email Expiration Manager

🌱 The Expires Header Initiative

The "Expires" email header is part of a broader environmental initiative to reduce the carbon footprint of email storage. Data centers storing billions of emails worldwide consume massive amounts of energy. Many emails have a natural expiration date but remain stored indefinitely, unnecessarily consuming resources.

Zero Carbon Email

The Zero Carbon Email initiative brings together email service providers, software vendors, and environmental advocates to address this issue. By introducing a standardized "Expires" header, email senders can indicate when their messages are no longer needed.

Standardization (IETF RFC)

The "Expires" header is currently being standardized through the Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF). The draft specification: draft-ietf-mailmaint-expires

This Extension's Role

Email Expiration Manager implements this emerging standard in Thunderbird, allowing you to automatically process emails according to sender-defined expiration dates. By using this extension, you're participating in making email more environmentally sustainable.
